## 😤 The Problem We're Solving

<!-- Add a problem image below -->
![Problem](./assets/problem.png)

> **"Students are missing classes just to stand in a fee payment queue."**

Every semester, students at Mahendra Institute of Technology face the same frustrating cycle:

- 🕐 **Long queues** at the accounts office waste hours of valuable time
- 📚 **Classes are missed** while waiting in line to pay fees
- 🗂️ **Manual challan processes** are slow, error-prone, and outdated
- 😓 **No real-time visibility** into pending fee amounts or fine details
- 📍 **Location-dependent** — students must physically visit the college to pay

**EduPay was built to eliminate all of this.**

---

## 💡 What is EduPay?

**EduPay** is a web-based fee payment platform designed exclusively for students. It allows you to view your fee details — including fine amounts — and pay instantly online using Net Banking, from anywhere, at any time.

No queues. No wasted time. No missed classes.

---

## ✨ Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| 🔍 **Smart Fee Lookup** | Enter your name, roll number, department & year to instantly view your fee breakdown |
| 💸 **Online Payment** | Pay using Net Banking — fast and secure |
| 🧾 **Instant Receipt** | Get your payment receipt immediately after successful payment |
| 📊 **Google Sheets Backend** | Fee and challan data managed seamlessly via Google Sheets |
| 📱 **Mobile Friendly** | Fully operable from your smartphone — no laptop needed |
| ⚡ **One-Click Payment** | Simple, minimal steps from login to payment confirmation |

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

```
Frontend       →  HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
Data Backend   →  Google Sheets (Challan Details)
Hosting        →  GitHub Pages
```

---

## 🖥️ How It Works

```
1. Open EduPay on your mobile or browser
        ↓
2. Enter your Name, Roll Number, Department & Year
        ↓
3. View your total fee details including any fine amount
        ↓
4. Proceed to pay via Net Banking
        ↓
5. Receive your payment receipt instantly ✅
```

---

## 🗂️ System Flow

```
Student  →  EduPay Portal  →  Google Sheets (Challan Details)
                                      ↓
                              Accountant Reviews
                                      ↓
                              Advisor Approves
                                      ↓
                          Payment Confirmed + Receipt Issued
```
